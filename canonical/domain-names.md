The RCPCH owns a number of domain names for its various web presences.

### TLD List
|TLD | Usage/Notes|
|--- | ---|
|`rcpch.ac.uk` | Used for live and production services. This is the main domain for all the RCPCH's web presence and as such conveys the trusted Collegiate reputation of the RCPCH.|
|`rcpch.tech` | Used for development, staging, testing, and utilities, as well as documentation and other non-production services.|
|`rcpch.digital` | recently (March 2025) registered by @RichardBurley and is probably going to become a key domain for the RCPCH's Digital team, especially as an 'on-ramp' for enquiries.|
|`rcpch.tv` | this is possibly going to be used for the [RCPCH ED/WR video project](https://forum.rcpch.tech/t/rcpch-tv-educational-videos-in-the-paediatric-ed/346/2)|
|`rcpch.us` | this is a domain for the RCPCH&Us team but it redirects to the main .ac.uk|
|`rcpch.digital` | Landing page for the Digital team more widely, not managed by the Incubator|


### People to contact
The primary contact for any changes to or queries about domain names is the RCPCH IT team. Allow a lead time of a couple of days for changes.

In an emergency @michael.barton also has access to DNS settings.

### Registration of new domains
In the first instance contact @michael.barton and @RichardBurley to confirm permission, then raise with the IT team for registration.

### Subdomain naming guidelines

* Keep them short
* Keep them descriptive
* Keep them as predictable as possible, following conventions or established patterns
