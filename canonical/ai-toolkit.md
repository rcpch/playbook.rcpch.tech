### OpenAI Platform
The RCPCH has an account with OpenAI Platform (ChatGPT models), details and credentials are in [this topic](https://forum.rcpch.tech/t/openai-platform-account/385) for security. Ask a member of @admins for access to the key.

### On-premise AI server
RCPCH has purchased a bare metal server with GPU for larger-scale experiments in AI and LLM work. It is located at RCPCH HQ in the server room. It is not yet provisioned but should be in use later in 2025.

### Discourse AI
A useful platform for quick demos and experiments in AI usage, which comes with a decent-looking, multi-user, 'demoable' and web-based GUI, is the forum platform Discourse. There is a [Discourse AI plugin](https://meta.discourse.org/t/discourse-ai/259214) which can do things like
* [AI bot](https://meta.discourse.org/t/enable-ai-bot/266012)
* [AI search](https://meta.discourse.org/t/enable-ai-search/282420)
* [AI spam detection](https://meta.discourse.org/t/discourse-ai-spam-detection/343541)
* Automation + AI
  * [AI triage](https://meta.discourse.org/t/discourse-ai-ai-triage/281227)
  * [Periodic summary reports](https://meta.discourse.org/t/discourse-ai-periodic-summary-reports/290236)
* [Helper](https://meta.discourse.org/t/enable-ai-helper/283943)
* [Related topics](https://meta.discourse.org/t/enable-related-topics/281807)
* [Sentiment](https://meta.discourse.org/t/enable-sentiment-analysis/259599)
* [Summarize](https://meta.discourse.org/t/discourse-ai-summarization/262711)
